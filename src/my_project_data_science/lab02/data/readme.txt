babyweights.csv — CHDS baby weights (the lecture's "baby weights" dataset)

1174 mother–baby pairs from the Child Health and Development Studies (CHDS),
Oakland, 1960–1967 (Kaiser Foundation health plan members).

Columns:
  Birth Weight                birth weight in ounces
  Gestational Days            length of the pregnancy in days
  Maternal Age                mother's age in years
  Maternal Height             mother's height in inches
  Maternal Pregnancy Weight   mother's pregnancy weight in pounds
  Maternal Smoker             True / False

Source: https://www.inferentialthinking.com/data/baby.csv
(textbook data of "Inferential Thinking", Data 8, UC Berkeley); line endings
converted to \n. Used in the W02 visualization lecture and in lab02.
